The studio

One developer, in Kuwait.

Alaskarbros is a one-person studio. Every game here was designed, built and shipped by one developer in Kuwait, in English and in Arabic.

The games are small on purpose. Each is single-player and built around one clear idea: a planet you build up, a store you hide in, a line you hold, a tribe you unite, a mind you keep sharp.

  • 1 developer
  • 100% single-player
