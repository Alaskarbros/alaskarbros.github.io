Support

  • App: Global Nations (a multiplayer strategy game of nations at war, for iPhone)
  • Requirements: iOS 18 or later, and an internet connection
  • Developer: Alaskarbros (Omar Aldhafeeri)
  • Contact: alaskarbros@gmail.com
  • Terms of Use: alaskarbros.github.io/globalnations/terms

Emails are usually answered within a few days. Please include your commander name, your iPhone model and your iOS version.

Reporting abuse

If another commander posts or sends something abusive, use Report on the post, the message or their profile, and Block to stop them reaching you. A report hides that item from you at once. We review every report within 24 hours, remove what breaks the Terms of Use, and suspend or ban the account that posted it. You can also email alaskarbros@gmail.com with the commander's name and what happened.

Common questions

Is the game portrait or landscape? Portrait only, on iPhone.

Does it need internet? Yes. Every nation shares one world, so the game is always online.

What languages are supported? Fifteen: English, Arabic, German, Spanish, French, Indonesian, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Thai, Turkish, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese.

Are there in-app purchases? Yes, one optional purchase: the Sovereign Fund, which adds cash to your treasury and civilians to your nation. Everything in the game can be played without it. Apple handles the payment; refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I paid but the Sovereign Fund did not arrive. Open the game again while you are online. It keeps retrying the delivery until our server confirms it, and a purchase is never delivered twice. If it still has not arrived after a day, email us with your commander name and the order ID from Apple's receipt.

Can I play without an account? Yes, as a guest. A guest nation lives on that iPhone only, so to keep it when you change or reset your phone, bind it in Settings with Sign in with Apple or an email address. Standing for your nation's seat and voting need a bound account.

I forgot my password. Email us from the address you signed up with, and we will help you back in.

Can I change my commander name? No. Names are permanent, so choose with care. A name that breaks the Terms of Use is replaced, and you are asked to choose again.

Where did my post go? Posts on the Comms boards last 24 hours. A post or message that breaks the word filter is refused, and the game tells you why.

How do I delete my account? In the game, open Settings and choose Delete account. It is immediate and permanent. Section 8 of the privacy policy below says what it removes. If you cannot sign in, email us from the address on the account.

Global Nations Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 October 2026

This policy explains what information is collected when you play Global Nations on your iPhone, why it is collected, who receives it, and the choices you have. Global Nations is made by Alaskarbros ("we", "us").

In short

  • You need an account to play, because every nation shares one world. You can sign up with an email address, sign in with Apple, or play as a guest with no email at all.
  • Other players see your commander name and nation, your rank and your battles, and the posts you make on the boards. They never see your email address or your votes.
  • What you write is stored on our server: posts, national addresses and mail. Board posts are deleted after 24 hours. We read what you write only when it is reported, or when we need to enforce the Terms of Use.
  • We record how the game is played (for example, which buildings you upgrade and how battles end) to balance and improve it. This stays with us. There are no ads, no third-party analytics and no tracking, and the game never asks for your advertising identifier.
  • Apple handles payments. We receive a record of what you bought, never your payment details.
  • We don't sell your personal information.
  • You can delete your account in Settings at any time.
  • Global Nations is not directed at children under 13.

1. Who we are

Alaskarbros makes Global Nations and is responsible for the data described here (the "controller" under European data protection law). Contact: alaskarbros@gmail.com. Website: https://alaskarbros.github.io

2. What we collect, and why

Your account. If you sign up with an email address, we store that address, and our hosting provider stores your password in scrambled (hashed) form; we never see it. If you sign in with Apple, Apple gives us an identifier for your account; the game does not ask Apple for your name or your email address. A guest account has only a random identifier. We use this to sign you in and to keep your nation yours.

Your commander and your nation. The name you choose, your nation and flag, and your game: treasury, civilians, buildings, units, battles, medals, levels and titles, alliances, elections, daily rewards and settings that the game keeps on its server. This is the game itself; without it there is nothing to play.

What you write. Posts on the Comms boards (your nation's, the global board and foreign boards), addresses you post as your nation's representative, and mail you send to other commanders. Before anything is published, our server checks it against lists of offensive words in each language and refuses it if it matches; no outside or AI service reads your messages. Mail is delivered to the commander you address.

Reports and blocks. When you report a post, a message or a commander, we store your report and a copy of what you reported, so we can review it. When you block a commander, we store that, so their mail stops reaching you.

Purchases. When you buy the Sovereign Fund, Apple processes the payment. The game sends Apple's signed record of the purchase to our server, which checks it and stores the transaction ID, the product, the date and what was added to your nation. We use it to deliver the purchase exactly once and to handle refund and support questions. We never receive your card or payment details.

How the game is played. The game records events such as starting a session (with the app's version and language), buying units, upgrading buildings, sending an attack, a battle's result, claiming a daily reward, and the app closing unexpectedly. These are linked to your account so we can see how the game plays over time, balance it and fix problems. They are not shared with anyone, not used for advertising, and not combined with data from other apps or websites.

Technical data. Like any online service, our hosting provider processes your IP address and basic details of each request (such as the time and the address requested) to deliver the game and keep it secure. These logs are kept for a short time.

On your iPhone. The game keeps your sign-in session and your preferences (such as language, sound and text size) on your device. If you allow notifications, reminders (for example, that a building has finished) are scheduled by the game on your iPhone; we do not send push notifications.

What we don't collect. Your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone and advertising identifier. When you found your nation, the game may suggest one from your iPhone's region setting; that suggestion is made on your device, and only the nation you choose is sent.

3. What other players can see

Your commander name, nation and flag; your title and level; your nation's standing, medals and rank on the leaderboards; battles you fight, in the news; your posts on the boards while they last; addresses you post as representative; and whether you stand for your nation's seat, and the result. Mail is seen only by you and the commander you write to. Other players never see your email address, your purchases or how you voted.

4. Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

We use your account, your game and what you write because we need them to provide the game you asked for (performance of a contract). We use reports, technical data and records of how the game is played because we have a legitimate interest in keeping the game safe, enforcing its rules, and understanding how it plays so we can improve it; you can object at any time by contacting us. We keep purchase records so that each purchase is delivered exactly once and refund questions can be answered (performance of a contract, and our legitimate interest in preventing fraud).

5. Who receives data

  • Supabase hosts the game's server, database and sign-in, and processes data only on our instructions. Its servers for this game are in Singapore (Amazon Web Services). Supabase's privacy policy: https://supabase.com/privacy
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple and handles in-app purchases under its own privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
  • Authorities, if the law requires it.

We have no advertising partners, analytics companies, data brokers or AI providers. If Global Nations is ever transferred to another company, this policy will keep applying to the data collected under it.

Your data is stored and processed outside your country, in Singapore. Where it leaves the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, we rely on Supabase's data processing agreement, which includes the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

6. How long data is kept

  • Your account, your nation and your game: until you delete your account.
  • Board posts: deleted 24 hours after posting.
  • Mail: no longer than the account that sent it.
  • Reports: kept as moderation records, with the copy of what was reported, for as long as we need them to keep the game safe.
  • Technical logs: a few days.
  • After you delete your account, some records stay under a random identifier, with no email address or name attached: purchase records (so a purchase can never be delivered twice and refund questions can be answered), records of how the game was played, battles you fought (with your name removed from the news), and a one-way fingerprint of your commander name, so that nobody else can take it and pose as you.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • ask what personal information we hold about you, and get a copy;
  • ask us to correct or delete it;
  • object to, or restrict, how we use it;
  • be treated the same when you use these rights;
  • complain to your data protection authority.

To use any of these rights, email us at alaskarbros@gmail.com from the address on your account, or tell us your commander name. We may ask for details that confirm the account is yours. We reply within one month (within 45 days for requests under US state laws).

8. Deleting your account

Open Settings in the game and choose Delete account. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. It removes your sign-in and email address, your commander name, your nation's capital and forces, your posts, the mail you sent, your blocks, and your place in elections and alliances. Section 6 lists the few records that are kept without your name. If you cannot sign in, email us from the address on the account and we will delete it for you.

9. Children

Global Nations is not directed at children under 13, and you must be at least 13 (or the age of digital consent where you live) to play. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under that age. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has an account, contact us at alaskarbros@gmail.com and we will delete it.

10. Security

Everything the game sends to our server travels encrypted (HTTPS). Every table in our database has access rules, so a player can reach only what the game shows them, and only the developer can reach the rest. No way of storing or sending data is completely secure.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how the game handles data, we will update this policy and its effective date. If a change is important, we will say so in the game or in the update notes before it takes effect.
